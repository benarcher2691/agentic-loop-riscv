## Contracts & limits (documented behaviour, not bugs)

- **Alignment**: until T2 lands, misaligned accesses return aligned data silently; after T2 they halt.
- **Reserved encodings**: unknown opcodes (incl. FENCE) are NOPs; unimplemented SYSTEM encodings halt permanently. There is no trap machinery and no soft reset — recovery from a halt is FPGA reconfiguration. Self-consistent with the power-on-only reset design.
- **UART RX**: last-writer-wins on overrun, no loss indication; the host must not send while the CPU cannot poll (inside `G` routines). `tools/hw.py` enforces pacing implicitly by its request-response usage.
- **UART TX**: data writes while the transmitter is busy are dropped (T4 pins this).
- **Monitor**: a partial command must be completed (GETBYTE blocks); a non-returning `G` routine hangs the monitor — host-side timeouts (`hw.py`) are the recovery. Programs and results live at 0x400/0x800; the stack at 0x1800 grows down from the top of RAM.
- **Address space**: 6 KB RAM, 13-bit data/PC addressing, mod-8192 wrap on jumps; IO at bit 22, exactly four IO words.
